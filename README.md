## 💖 About GymTracker

GymTracker is a beautifully simple workout routine tracker designed to keep you motivated and aware of your progress. Originally developed in TypeScript with AI Studio and brought to life via Android Studio, this app goes beyond standard logging by actually showing you the impact of your workouts.

### ✨ What makes it special?

* 🔥 **Dynamic Muscle Heatmap:** Watch your progress glow! The app generates a visual heatmap of your body, with colors intensifying proportionally to the load and volume you put into specific muscle groups. 
* 🌟 **Streak Tracking:** Consistency is key. Keep your workout streaks alive in our dedicated streak section and watch your daily motivation soar.
